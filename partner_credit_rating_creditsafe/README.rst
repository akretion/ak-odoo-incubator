Creditsafe provider for ``partner_credit_rating``: company
identification and company credit report with the Creditsafe Connect
API.

The international score sets the risk: A is low, B medium, C high and D
critical (E, not rated, has no risk). The credit limit advised by
Creditsafe is shown on the rating.

Adapted from ``coreff_creditsafe`` (Article714).

Configuration
=============

In *Settings > General Settings > Contacts > Credit Rating*, select
Creditsafe then fill the environment, username and password.

Companies are searched by Company ID (SIRET / SIREN for France), then by
tax ID, then by name and city.
